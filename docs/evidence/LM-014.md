# LM-014: baseline snapshots, proposal status and temporal applicability

The library resolves dated baseline variants by jurisdiction/agency, explicit official-source snapshot records, staged effective dates, declared transition periods and the optional assumption of hypothetical enactment. Drafts, introduced bills and future legislative changes remain "not enacted" by default even when simulated dates exceed proposed effective dates. Enactment assumptions are explicitly labeled in the result and never change the recorded legislative status.

An official source snapshot must have a pinned digest, version label, official HTTPS origin, declared as-of date, citation and review verification. A vacant source inventory is not authoritative. Missing baseline material remains a structured gap rather than a guessed current-law rule, and different agency/jurisdiction scope produces an unresolved variant. Structural validation rejects bad dates, future snapshots, duplicate IDs, inconsistent transition intervals and unsupported sources.

Tests cover before/after transitions and enactment, missing source gaps, agency variants, temporal scope errors, invalid official URLs and duplicate law changes.

## Pinned official-source excerpts and coverage limitations

Two separate public-domain **2024 Edition** U.S. Government Publishing Office sources are included as curated, byte-pinned *short excerpts*, not complete GPO HTML/PDF files:

- 44 U.S.C. §3506(a)(1) — excerpt "The head of each agency shall be responsible for", identified at https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title44/html/USCODE-2024-title44-chap35-subchapI-sec3506.htm. The archived excerpt bytes are stored at content/baselines/electronic-filing/USCODE-2024-title44-3506-excerpt.txt and have SHA-256 972ee3237056092053b3e8f1d668db19e8ae0277b2bee909f109e1d5a0cc012b.
- 5 U.S.C. §552(a)(3)(A) — excerpt "shall make the records promptly available to any person", identified at https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title5/pdf/USCODE-2024-title5-partI-chap5-subchapII-sec552.pdf. Archived excerpt bytes are stored at content/baselines/electronic-filing/USCODE-2024-title5-552-excerpt.txt and have SHA-256 dbba02fd51861948b44d248c33f041bdcdffe1fec1df08102559b91c613b3243.

The two recorded hashes authenticate these **locally transcribed excerpts**, not the original GPO HTML/PDF bytes or the currentness/accuracy of the whole source. Automated tests hash the literal excerpt files and compare source records, so changed curated bytes invalidate the snapshot. The versions are explicitly dated 2024, while the 2026 baseline remains marked **incomplete**: amendments since 2024, agency-specific procedures, and the complete proposed electronic-filing bill text are not supplied. Neither excerpt is compiled into a legal rule or treated as sufficient to decide an actual claim. This partial source inventory and explicit unmodeled gaps satisfy the *limited initial snapshot representation*, not product-level legal baseline acceptance. The platform must obtain and verify complete current official source artifacts before declaring the first comparison pack authoritative.


Security: no network or provider dependency, arbitrary file access, executable rule language, secret, external write or implicit publication. Rollback is a checked revert; no migrations. Required exact-head foundation/workspace checks and browser, type, audit and build gates. An AI-assisted review is not independent external certification.
