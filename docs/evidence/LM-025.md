# LM-025 operational queue — first slice

Implements a bounded, deterministic, assumption-labeled capacity queue whose processing records remain distinct from normative due dates. Work arrival, whole service units, resource capacity and completed work are explicit. Tests check higher workload can cause overdue performance without modifying legal deadlines, exact integer capacity accounting, labels, malformed data and duplicates.

This implementation now also models explicit outage ticks and one-time failure/retry interventions, with tests verifying legal deadlines remain unchanged. Empirical/stochastic service distributions and richer compliance models are future scope; declared fixed rates are assumptions or sourced empirical inputs rather than statutory rules. It is not a legal rule compiler, legal filing service or real queue measurement. No credentials, external services or migrations. Merge only after acceptance and required CI; rollback via reviewed revert.
