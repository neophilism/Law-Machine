# LM-023 duties, rights, permissions, prohibition and breach

Implements normative status records and a provider-free obligation state evaluator. Records distinguish a legal duty's existence from observed actor performance, distinguish timely satisfaction from post-deadline breach, keep permissions inert rather than automatically causing behavior, and refuse remedies without their own applicable source-linked rule. A receipt is evidence of the specified performance only; it does not produce a merits judgment.

Unit tests cover two-tick duty/five-tick completion, missing performance, permissions, source-supported remedies, and invalid provenance. Actual calendar counting belongs to LM-022; conversion of dated due rules into scheduler ticks must be explicitly supplied by consumers. This is not real legal advice and all examples are fictional. No credentials, persistence, legal filing or deployment. Rollback: revert this PR after disabling downstream dependents.
